Attendance Percentage Checker
A simple, user-friendly web tool designed to help students track and manage their college attendance effectively. Many colleges don't provide real-time percentage updates; this tool fills that gap.

📝 Why I Built This
In my college, attendance percentages aren't regularly disclosed to students. I built this for myself and my friends to ensure we stay on track with university requirements and can plan our leaves or "catch-up" days without any guesswork.

🚀 Features
1.The calculator goes beyond just showing a percentage. It provides actionable insights based on your current status:

2.Attendance Recovery: If your attendance is below the required threshold (e.g., 75% or 80%), the tool calculates exactly how many consecutive classes you must attend to reach your goal.

3.Leave Planner: If you have a high attendance record (85% - 95%), the tool calculates how many days you can safely miss while still maintaining a healthy percentage (above 80%).

4.Real-time Calculation: Get instant results by entering your total conducted days and days absent.

🛠️ How to Use:

Enter the Total days the college has run so far until now.

Enter the Number of days you were absent.

Click Calculate.

View your current percentage and the custom advice generated based on your stats.
